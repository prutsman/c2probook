Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How much does bookkeeping cost?
It depends on your transaction volume and how complex your books are. Every business is different, so you'll get a clear, upfront quote during your free consultation before you commit to anything.
My books are a mess and months behind. Can you still help?
Yes, this is one of the most common reasons clients come to us. We regularly clean up and catch up backlogged books.
Do I have to switch accounting software?
No. We're Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisors and work within QuickBooks Online or Desktop, whatever you're already using.
Can I see my own books, any time?
Yes. Your books live in QuickBooks Online, so you can log in and see exactly where things stand from home, the office, or your phone.
I'm not in Denver. Can we still work together?
Most of our bookkeeping is handled remotely through QuickBooks Online, so location isn't a barrier for most clients.
Is my financial information secure?
Yes. Your books and records are kept accurate, current, and secure, and we work directly with your CPA or tax preparer so nothing falls through the cracks.
